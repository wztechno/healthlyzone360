Before you start

  • Everything here is invented. Every kitchen, meal, person and price is synthetic. No real business or customer appears anywhere.
  • No email is delivered. Password resets and verification links are written to the server log instead of being sent. Ask for them if you need one.
  • No payment is taken. Checkout completes without contacting a payment provider. No card is charged.
  • Breaking things is fine. Demo records are recreated on every deploy, so you cannot damage anything permanently.

Consumer buyer

Nour Haddad · self-service customer, Dubai

Start here
nour@healthy360.test

Somebody shopping for themselves. This account has a verified email, an unverified phone, and — deliberately — a saved address inside an area Verdant Kitchen actually delivers to, so the marketplace, the plan configurator and checkout all have something deliverable to work with.

  1. Browse the marketplace before signing in, from the home page.Four kitchens are listed, and you can open any of them and read a menu without an account.
  2. Open Verdant Kitchen, then View the menu.Each meal card carries its energy, protein and allergens on the card itself — not hidden behind a tap.
  3. Check the kitchen's delivery zones.Two: Emirates wide, about 90 minutes, $15.00 delivery, $50.00 minimum; and Al Quoz express, about 30 minutes, $25.00 delivery, $30.00 minimum.
  4. Sign in, then add meals to a basket and go through checkout.Your saved Al Quoz address is offered and accepted. The order completes without any payment step charging you.
  5. Open a subscription plan and run the configurator.You can set a target, pick a plan, and reach a price — the weekly figures move when you change the configuration.
  6. Use the search and the cuisine and delivery filters on the kitchens list.The result count updates and only matching kitchens remain.

Already known — no need to report

Known limitations of this test instance

  • Marketplace cards are clipped on phones. On screens narrower than about 437 px the right edge of each kitchen card is cut off. Known layout bug, already logged.
  • Email never arrives. Anything that would send a message writes it to the server log instead.
  • Payments are simulated. Checkout finishes without a real provider being contacted.
  • Some analytics figures are sample data, and say so on screen.
  • Everything is a prototype. No terms, privacy notice or licence has been published, so none is linked. Nothing here is an offer.
