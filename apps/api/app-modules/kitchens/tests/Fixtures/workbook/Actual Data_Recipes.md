<!--
    SYNTHETIC FIXTURE. Nine invented technical sheets.

    The raw-material designations are ordinary foodstuffs — mayonnaise, salt,
    parsley — and some are deliberately written in the misspelled forms the
    curated alias dictionary maps ("Cripsy Spice", "Mustard Dijon", "Red
    Vineagar", "Onion White", "Liquid Milk", "White Sugar"), so the importer's
    resolution path is exercised rather than stubbed. One designation,
    "Fixture Unknown Spice", is deliberately unresolvable: the report-and-
    continue path is a tested behaviour and needs a line that cannot resolve.

    Sheet 7 is designated "Sour Cream" and sheet 9 consumes it as a raw
    material. That makes it an intermediate — a thing this kitchen produces
    and then uses — which is what the outputs table exists to record, and it
    is decided from the data rather than from a list.

    What the private workbook actually protects is invented here and nowhere
    copied: every preparation name, every quantity, every unit price, every
    total and every yield. The structure — the header block, the merged notice
    rows, the raw-material block, the Total row, the cost block — mirrors the
    source. The numbers do not.
-->
