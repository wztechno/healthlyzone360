# Lane 4: food safety follow-through and P1 Wave B

Date: 3 November 2026. Branch P0-Stage1. Migrations `2026_11_03_04xxxx`.

## Built

1. **Quarantine.** A quality hold now sets stock aside. `StockQuarantine` opens a `stock_reservations` row (holder `quality_hold`, holder id the receipt or batch) for what the subject brought in, read from its own stock movements and capped at the shelf. Because it is a claim, every existing availability rule stops it: `recordMovement('consume')`, batch confirm and complete, the planner and the buy list. Release closes the claim and retries the blocked order lines. Hold and release are audited with who did it. A sale that could come only from held stock is refused `quality_hold` in `LineProbe`, so cart, checkout, desk and placement all agree. An order already placed meets the hold at the deduction as a new `quality_hold` stock gap, whose fix link opens quality control.
2. **Expiry alerts.** `AlertExpiringStock`, daily at 05:00 UTC, tells managers through `StaffAlerts` about finished batches and delivery lines dated within two days (or up to seven days past), with stock still on the shelf. Once per batch or line. `StaffAlertType` gained `subjectType()` and `page()`, so an alert need not be about an order; `stock_expiring` opens `/kitchen/stock`.
3. **Dish options.** `catalogue_item_options`: `remove` and `choice` (with a group), free by construction, no amount column. Edited on the meal's listing. Customers choose on the meal page, the desk from a drawer on each ticket line. Chosen options are checked and snapshotted on the line (`selected_options`). A choice from one group only; a withdrawn option is refused `option_unknown` at placement.
4. **Portions.** A portion is a pack. The meal page and the desk list a dish's priced packs when there are at least two, the line carries `catalogue_item_variant_id`, and pricing is the existing price list. No new price field.
5. **Line notes.** Customers and the desk. The meal page's special-instructions box was a stub; it now sends. Shown on the kitchen display, the order and the desk.
6. **Same dish twice.** `line_key` joins both unique indexes (`cart_items_one_row_per_line`, `order_lines_one_row_per_article`). It is empty on a plain line, so plain lines merge exactly as before, and a hash of options and note otherwise.
7. **Complaints.** `order_complaints`: cause, description, cost, outcome, links to a refund and a remake. List and record at `/kitchen/complaints`, opened from the order page. **Remake** places a real order through `placeComposed` with every line at `PriceOverride::REMAKE`, keeping options, notes and the allergy note, delivery fee waived, confirmed at once.
8. **Temperature log.** `temperature_units`, `temperature_readings` (once per unit, day and slot, written once, day and person stamped by the server, `out_of_range` stored), `cooking_temperatures`. `/kitchen/temperature-logs`, by month, printed through the existing list print. Out of range alerts managers. Readings are writable by `order.prepare_organisation` or `inventory.manage_organisation`, so a cook can take one with no new permission.

## Decisions

- **The 13 October "warn, never refuse" ruling is narrowed, not reversed.** This brief says a held batch cannot be sold. Stock is one number per shelf, so the hold bites at quantity grain: a held delivery of oil sets that delivery's kilos aside and last week's oil stays on sale, with the desk's existing warning. The sale is refused only when free stock cannot cover it. Refusing every dish containing a held ingredient would have taken the menu down over one delivery.
- The holder is the subject, not the check, so two checks on one delivery set it aside once, and the claim stands until the last is released.
- A hold is never refused, so it writes past `ReservationService::open()`. A shelf can read short; `isShort()` already says so.
- To throw held food away: record waste, then release. Release frees only what is left. The copy now says this, and that the food must also be moved by hand.
- The quality-hold check shares `$enforceAssessment`: a subscription delivery and a line an edited order already holds are not refused, and meet the hold at the deduction.
- Sale check is summed across branches because a sale often names none. A kitchen with free stock at another branch passes and meets the hold at the deduction. For finished stock it asks only whether anything is free, since one unit's size is the consume path's arithmetic.
- Choice groups are optional, not required: every path that adds a dish with no options (grid Add, subscriptions, quotations) must keep working.
- Removing an ingredient changes no allergen data. No allergen code was touched.
- Options read open and write under RLS, as `branch_closures`: a menu is public and a basket reads it with no organisation declared.
- Portions show only when two or more packs are priced on the channel. The desk shows counter prices; the quote stays the authority for a customer's own price list.
- `cost_minor` on a complaint is what the kitchen says. The remake's food cost reaches COGS through the ordinary deduction and is not added to it.
- A remake uses the original's channel, skips the day and slot checks (`enforceWindowOffer: false`, as subscription generation does) and is acknowledged for allergens because the first order was. A delivery goes to the saved address matching the snapshot, or the customer's latest; none refuses with a pointer to the desk.
- Complaints need `order.supervise_organisation`: no new permission.
- Readings have no edit path. An error stays on the record with a note on the next reading.
- Expiry window is a constant, two days, not a kitchen setting.

## Not done

- **Lot-level stock and recall traceability.** Left as instructed. Quarantine is quantity-grain until it exists, so which kilos are held is the kitchen's to know.
- Cooling and delivery logs: the owner did not choose them. No missed-reading alert, no minimum core temperature rule: neither was asked for.
- Options and notes on the customer's own order page and printed receipt: the API serves them on customer and guest lines; the screens were not changed.
- No paid add-ons, by ruling.
- A remake's order page does not name the original; the complaint record holds the link.
- Tests are written and not run, per the brief.
