# Lane 1: P0 Stage 2 and the order desk customers

Date: 3 November 2026. Built overnight on `P0-Stage1`. Tests are written and not run, per the brief.

## Built

1. **Order desk customers.** The customer comes first on New Order, above the menu, which stays shut until a customer or an explicit Walk-in is chosen. Declared allergens show on the chosen customer's card, and conflicting dishes get a danger border, icon and wording on the tile. Registered customers are found. Saved delivery addresses can be chosen for customers in the kitchen's book. A local number (`70 123 456`) gets the kitchen's code before searching.
2. **Service brakes.** Off today, one press on the desk tile. A timed Pause from the desk toolbar. Capacity per delivery slot on `/kitchen/branch-operating`. `remaining_portions` counts down at placement and back on cancel.
3. **Customer notices.** Email and SMS on confirmed, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled, failed delivery.
4. **Prototype language removed.** Footer, Reorder, allergies disclaimer, `noPayment` line, closed-day pill. `/legal/[page]` exists and `LEGAL_PAGES` is empty.
5. **Truthful margin.** Revenue is food only. Margin is withheld on the cost report with a badge and a record note.

## Decisions

**Customers**
- B2C accounts were excluded by both layers. A registered consumer with an order here was always found. One with no order here failed the query rule and read as no row under RLS, so widening the query alone would have done nothing.
- A seventh arm was added, and it is narrow: a `kitchen_customer_links` row. An exact E.164 number written by the desk search creates the link, because a caller who reads out their number has identified themselves. The arm carries the other six, and is SELECT only.
- Nothing searches consumers by name across the platform. "Any registered consumer" would give every kitchen a directory of the marketplace, with allergies one press away. So a never-ordered customer is found by full number only, and after that by name too.
- The number need not be verified, since no SMS channel exists yet. The risk: a person who typed someone else's number at sign-up is found on that number.
- The link write sits behind `GET`: one idempotent, audited row. A separate endpoint would only move the same disclosure a tap later.
- The book takes no link arm. A number read over the phone opens neither saved addresses nor emails until the customer has ordered here.
- Subscribers were added to the desk rule. RLS already admitted them and the query did not.
- Allergies use `DietaryProfileReader` with `OrderFulfilment`, once per chosen customer and never per search row. "Never asked" reads differently from "no allergens", and does not read as safe.
- A counter sale may now carry a named customer, which the wire always allowed. Edit is hidden on a registered customer, because the row policy refuses that write.

**Brakes**
- Pause and capacity refuse customers. The desk gets a warning on the quote and goes ahead: that is the override. No tick box.
- Subscriptions and accepted quotations are exempt from the brakes and from portions, but they do fill a window.
- A window counts every non-cancelled order, fulfilled included, not only open ones. A fulfilled order took its place on the line. This is a small departure from "open orders".
- Capacity is one number per kitchen. An order with no date or no window fills no window.
- Concurrency: capacity takes an advisory lock after the insert, then re-counts, so the second of two waits and rolls back. Portions use one conditional `UPDATE`, in item id order.
- Wasted cancellations give no portions back: the food is gone. `orders.portions_counted` stops cancels inventing stock for orders that took nothing.
- Amending an order releases and re-takes its portions.
- Off today sits on the desk tile for `catalogue.manage_organisation`, today only, any item type. Pause needs `order.supervise_organisation`, and is capped at 12 hours.

**Notices**
- Language: the account's, then the person's, then the kitchen's default.
- Counter sales hear nothing. A pickup hears confirmed and cancelled only.
- The job carries two ids. Mail goes with `sendNow()`, because `NoticeMessage` queues itself and would put the address on the queue.
- `tries = 1`: a lost notice is better than a doubled one.
- A close-as-failed tells the customer twice, failed then cancelled. Both are true.
- A guest email has no link, because their order page needs their session.

**Margin**
- Withheld when a month has a consumption exception, a sold line that drew no costed stock, or an unvalued batch.
- A missing estimate alone does not withhold it. That says what was expected, and the line may still carry a real cost.
- The owner took the issue tray off this page on 2 November, so the explanation is a row badge and the record note, not a card.
- Analytics carries no money figures, so it needed no change.

## Skipped

- **Per-window capacity.** Add it to `delivery_windows` when a kitchen asks.
- **Pause on the storefront.** Customers hear at checkout and placement, not while browsing.
- **Off today from `/kitchen/recipes`.** The row has no calendar state.
- **Other "not available yet" controls:** cart promo, pickup option, courier message, get help, planner share and export. The wording no longer says prototype, but the controls remain.
- **Calculator prototype wording** in `catalogue.json`, and synthetic-data copy on dietitians. Not in this lane's list.
- **Legal text.** The owner supplies it. `isPathAvailable` must also allow `/legal/*` when the first page is published.
